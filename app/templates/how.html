How it works

You choose a small sample of your video taste. We turn exactly that sample into a roast. Nothing else is read, fetched or guessed.

  1. Add a sample

    Type or paste at least {{ s.min_items }} video titles, channel names or interests. You can add up to {{ s.max_submitted_items }}. We never open links, so paste the title, not the URL.

  2. Review exactly what is sent

    You see every item, can edit or remove any of them, and see the exact list that will go to the AI provider. If you add more than {{ s.max_model_items }}, a fixed, balanced selection is made and shown to you.

  3. Pick an intensity

    Gentle, Spicy or Savage. Savage is funnier, not less safe: every safety rule applies at every level.

  4. Approve and generate

    You confirm the sample, accept the disclosures, and we send only the selected text to {{ provider }}. The result is checked for structure, for evidence, and for safety before you see it.

  5. Read, share, delete

    Every joke has a "Why this joke?" panel showing which of your items it came from. You can download a share card that contains only a persona and one quote, never your source items. Delete everything at any time, or let it expire after {{ s.report_ttl_hours }} hours.

What it is not

  • Not your watch history. YouTube does not expose it, and we never ask for it.
  • Not a personality test, a psychological assessment or a screen-time measurement.
  • Not an official YouTube product.
