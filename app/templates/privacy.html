Privacy

You choose the input. We send only your selected text to our AI provider. Your report is private and expires automatically.

Policy version {{ s.policy_version }} · Provider disclosure version {{ s.provider_disclosure_version }}

What we handle

  • The sample you type (titles, channels, interests) and the report generated from it. Both are encrypted in our database.
  • Your consent choices with timestamps and the policy versions you accepted.
  • Optional feedback, stored separately from your report.
  • A session cookie (one, strictly necessary, HttpOnly). No accounts, no email, no device fingerprinting.
  • Operational data: request logs without any sample or report text, a salted hash of your network address for rate limiting (kept about 2 days, never the address itself), and anonymous event counts.

What we do not do

  • We never access your YouTube account or watch history, and we never open links you paste.
  • No advertising trackers. No selling or sharing data for advertising.
  • We don't use your sample, reports or feedback to train AI models.
  • Prompts and outputs are never sent to error trackers or analytics.

Deleting your data

Use Delete my data on your results page at any time. Access stops immediately and your active data is removed within about a minute. Backups age out within 7 days, and we keep a record that a deletion happened (never what it contained) so deleted data can't reappear after a restore. We can't recall images you already downloaded.
