Safety and what we won't roast

Roasts target your viewing patterns, never you. These rules apply at every intensity.

Fair game

  • Contradictions between interests
  • Tutorial collecting and productivity binges
  • Overly specific hobbies and repeated themes
  • Playful, clearly fictional viewing personas

Never allowed in a result

  • Slurs, hate, or attacks on protected characteristics
  • Guessing religion, caste, ethnicity, sexuality, disability or politics
  • Diagnoses, or claims about addiction, criminality or danger
  • Body-shaming, sexual humiliation, threats, or self-harm encouragement
  • Invented facts about your job, money, relationships or family
  • Insulting the creators you watch
  • Claims to know your real watch history

How we enforce it

  1. Before generation: we flag items that look sensitive (health, religion, politics, sexuality, personal details and similar) and leave them out of what is sent. You can see and edit them. This screening is a best effort and cannot catch everything, so please don't submit anything you'd rather keep private.
  2. After generation: every result is checked for structure, for evidence that really comes from your sample, and against our safety rules. Unsafe results are never shown. We retry once with a gentler instruction; if that also fails you get a plain failure message, not a generic insult passed off as personal.

Roast only yourself

Only submit your own taste. This service is not for targeting or harassing other people.
